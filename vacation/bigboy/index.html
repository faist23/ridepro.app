One-car option worth discussing

We may benefit if one car skips the first stop and heads straight to Rocky River around noon — parking near the depot will be the scarcest resource of the day, and an early car could claim spaces at Beachcliff or Heinen's and hold a front-row spot for everyone at the whistle stop. The trade-off is missing the river crossing. Something to decide as a group.

Timing reality check

4014's Rocky River arrival is pinned at 1:00 PM, and downtown is only ~8 rail miles east of the depot — so the Cuyahoga crossing likely lands closer to noon than to 10:30. Arriving by 11:15 is still right (better an hour early than five minutes late). Bring patience, water, and shade.

What we're chasing

About No. 4014

Built by the American Locomotive Company in 1941 to haul wartime freight over the Wasatch grades between Utah and Wyoming, retired in 1961, displayed in California for half a century, then hauled back to Cheyenne and returned to steam in 2019. Twenty-five were built; eight survive; only this one runs. It's touring coast to coast for America's 250th — and Monday it comes to us.

Safety Notes

Stay back 25 feet from all tracks — that includes tripods and phones on selfie sticks. It applies at the overlook and the depot alike.
Always expect a train. These are active lines; never assume a track is quiet just because 4014 hasn't arrived yet.
No trespassing on tracks, trestles, or railroad right-of-way — the overlook and depot viewing areas are the legal vantage points.
Don't pace the train while driving the transfer to Rocky River. It'll be there, stationary, for a full half hour.
